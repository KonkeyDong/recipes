# Chicken and Bisquick Dumplings

This recipe is a **one-pot recipe**; it only dirties up one pot while preparing/cooking.

# Ingredients

* **8 Cups** chicken broth
* **1.25 Lbs.** boneless skinless chicken thighs
* **2 Cans** of Cream of Chicken soup
* **2 Cups** Carrots`*`
* **2 Cups** Celery`*`
* **2 Cups** White Onion`*`
* **8-12** small Red Potatoes`*`
* **1 Package** of Bisquick Pancake Mix`**`
   * **2 Cups** Bisquick Mix
   * **2/3 Cups** Milk
   * **1 Tablespoon** Vegetable Oil

## Ingredients Notes

* `*`: If you're feeling lazy or have a cat that likes to "help" you cut vegetables, frozen vegetables work well. Substitute all the fresh veggies mentioned above with two pounds of **Frozen Stew Vegetables**. Look for the ones that have **Carrots**, **White Onion**, **Celery**, and **Potatoes**.
* `**`: You can substitue **Bisquick Pancake Mix** for **Pillsbury Biscuits** Instead. Just get a pack of **10 Original Biscuits** and cut each biscuit into a 3x3 grid to produce 9 bite-size dumplings.
   
# Recipe

1. Add chicken thighs to a (6+ Quart) dutch oven and cover the chicken in broth; cover the dutch oven and bring to a boil; **reduce to a simmer and cook for 30 minutes**.
1. Shred the chicken in the broth.
1. Wash and add red potatoes **with the skins on**; **simmer 10 minutes**
   1. If using frozen stew vegetables, you can skip this step.
1. Cup up celery, carrots, and onion and add rest of broth to the dutch oven; **simmer 10 minutes**.
   1. If using frozen stew vegetables, the time remains **10 minutes**. Keep in mind that dumping frozen items into a boiling soup will decrease the soup's temperature quite a bit and takes a few moments to bring back tup to boiling temperatures.
1. Add both cans of cream of chicken soup.
1. Mix Bisquick dumplings; add dumplings by the spoonful (should get about 8 spoonfuls) to the soup; **cover the dutch oven and simmer for 10 minutes**; **remove lid and simmer for an additional 10 minutes**.
   1. If using **Pillsbury Biscuits**, dump them into the stew, bring to a simmer and cover the dutch oven for **10 minutes**. <ins>**You do not need to simmer uncovered for an additional 10 minutes**</ins>.

# Note

When storing the soup, take the **Bisquick Dumplings** out and store in a separate container from the soup. Otherwise, the dumplings will disintegrate into the soup. The Pillsbury dumpings should be refrigerated with the stew.
